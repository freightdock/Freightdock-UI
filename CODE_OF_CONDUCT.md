# Code of conduct

Freightdock follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, argue about code and not about people, and leave out harassment,
insults and personal attacks of any kind. This holds in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else you speak
for the project.

To report a problem with somebody's behaviour, open a private advisory through the repository's **Security** tab
(as in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and say it is about conduct. Reports are kept private. The maintainer may remove
comments, close threads or block people who do not follow this.
